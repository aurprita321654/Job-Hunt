How the search actually goes

Four stages, in order. Most people are stuck in stage one longer than they realize.

  1. 01

    Map the target

    We narrow "something in marketing" down to five real job titles and the companies actually hiring for them right now, so every hour you spend applying is aimed at something specific.

  2. 02

    Build the kit

    Resume, LinkedIn, and a short list of talking points get rebuilt around that target — not a one-size-fits-all template, a set of materials built for the roles you picked.

  3. 03

    Practice the terrain

    Ahead of every interview, you get a detailed summary and notes on the role and what's likely to come up — so you walk in prepared instead of guessing.

  4. 04

    Close it out

    When an offer lands, you've already got the materials, the applications, and the prep behind you — we close things out and you start the new role ready.
